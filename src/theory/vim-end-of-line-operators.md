# Operators to end of line

Three shorthands for "act from here to the end of the line," so common
they get their own single-letter commands instead of an operator+motion:

- `C` — change to end of line (short for `c$`)
- `D` — delete to end of line (short for `d$`)
- `Y` — yank the whole line (behaves like `yy`, not `y$`)

`C` and `D` both mean "to end of line"; `Y` is the odd one out, worth
remembering separately.
