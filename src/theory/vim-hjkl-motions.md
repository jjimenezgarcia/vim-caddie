# Basic movement: h j k l

Four keys, one per direction, all on the home row:

- `h` — left
- `j` — down
- `k` — up
- `l` — right

They do the same thing as the arrow keys, just without moving your hand
off the home row to reach them — arrows still work, they're just slower
to get to every single time you want to move. Prefix any of them with a
count to repeat: `3j` moves down three lines.
