# Paragraph and sentence motions

Four motions for jumping between larger chunks of text than a word,
without needing to search for anything:

- `}` — forward to the next blank line (end of paragraph)
- `{` — backward to the previous blank line (start of paragraph)
- `)` — forward to the start of the next sentence
- `(` — backward to the start of the current/previous sentence

Like any motion, they combine with operators: `d}` deletes to the end
of the paragraph.
